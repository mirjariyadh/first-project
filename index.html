01 — About

Precision modeling for teams
who build for real.

I turn drawings, point clouds, and half-finished CAD files into coordinated, clash-free BIM models — the kind that hold up on site, not just in a render.

Over the past several years I've worked inside architecture, structural, and MEP workflows for studios and contractors across multiple countries, taking projects from raw survey data through to construction-ready documentation.

My approach is simple: model at LOD the project actually needs, keep families clean and parametric, and hand back a file a team can keep working in — not one they have to fix first.
